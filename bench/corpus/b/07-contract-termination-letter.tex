\textbf{Re: Notice of Termination of Facility Services Agreement dated March 1, 2023}\\[\medskipamount]
Dear Mr.\ Corrigan:}

This letter is formal written notice that Northfield Logistics, Inc.\ (``Northfield'') is terminating the Facility Services Agreement between Northfield and Brightline Facility Services LLC (``Brightline'') dated March 1, 2023, together with all statements of work issued under it (collectively, the ``Agreement''). Northfield is exercising its right to terminate for convenience under Section 12.2 of the Agreement, which permits either party to terminate on sixty (60) days' prior written notice. This notice is delivered in accordance with Section 15.1 (Notices).

\textbf{Effective date.} The termination will take effect at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on \textbf{Tuesday, December 8, 2026} (the ``Termination Date''). Until then, we ask that Brightline continue to perform all services at the Grand Rapids distribution center and the Kentwood cross-dock facility in accordance with the Agreement and Statement of Work No.~3, including the service levels in Exhibit B.

\textbf{Transition and close-out.} Under Sections 9.4, 12.5 and 7.3 of the Agreement, we request the following:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Transition assistance.} Reasonable cooperation for up to thirty (30) days after the Termination Date with Northfield's successor provider, including a walkthrough of each facility and a handover meeting with our Facilities Director no later than November 20, 2026.
  \item \emph{Records.} Delivery by the Termination Date of all equipment maintenance logs, inspection records, safety data sheets, and open work orders relating to Northfield facilities.
  \item \emph{Property and access.} Return of all keys, access cards, and Northfield-owned equipment and supplies, with a signed inventory, by the Termination Date. Northfield will deactivate all Brightline access credentials at that time.
  \item \emph{Confidential information.} Return or destruction of Northfield confidential information, with written certification of destruction signed by an officer of Brightline within fifteen (15) days after the Termination Date.
  \item \emph{Final invoice.} Submission of a final invoice covering services performed through the Termination Date within thirty (30) days after that date. Northfield will pay all undisputed amounts on the Agreement's standard net-45 terms. Section 12.2 provides that no early-termination fee is payable on termination for convenience.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Surviving terms.} The provisions of the Agreement that by their terms survive termination remain in effect, including Section 9 (Confidentiality), Section 10 (Indemnification), Section 11 (Limitation of Liability), and the insurance obligations in Section 13.3. Nothing in this letter waives or limits any right or remedy available to Northfield under the Agreement or applicable law, all of which are expressly reserved.

Please direct operational questions about the transition to Daniel Ruiz, Facilities Director, at (616) 555-0174 or \href{mailto:druiz@example.com}{druiz@example.com}. Questions about the Agreement itself may be sent to me directly.

We appreciate the work Brightline's team has done at our facilities over the past three and a half years, and we intend to make the transition orderly for both companies. Kindly acknowledge receipt of this notice by signing below and returning a copy to my attention.
