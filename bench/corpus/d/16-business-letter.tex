\opening{Dear Mr.\ Reyes:}

\textbf{Re: Supply Agreement No.\ CPC-SA-2024-017 --- Notice of Delivery and Quality Nonconformance}

I am writing to formally notify Westbrook Castings, Inc.\ (``Westbrook'') of continuing delivery and quality problems under the above Supply Agreement, dated March 1, 2024, and to request a written corrective action plan.

Over the past two quarters, Cardinal Precision Components, Inc.\ (``Cardinal'') has experienced the following issues with ductile iron housings (part numbers CPC-4471 and CPC-4472) supplied by Westbrook:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Late deliveries.} Of the 26 scheduled shipments between April 1 and September 30, 2026, 11 arrived after the confirmed delivery date, by between 3 and 12 business days. Section~4.2 of the Agreement requires on-time delivery of at least 95\%; actual performance was 58\%.
  \item \textbf{Nonconforming material.} Our incoming inspection rejected 1,940 of 31,200 housings received in the same period (6.2\%), principally for porosity in the mounting bosses and bore diameters outside the drawing tolerance of 62.000--62.046~mm. Section~6.1 sets a maximum rejection rate of 1.0\%.
  \item \textbf{Corrective action responses.} Westbrook has not yet responded to Supplier Corrective Action Requests SCAR-2026-031 (issued June 12) and SCAR-2026-044 (issued August 19), although Section~6.4 requires a response within 15 business days.
\end{enumerate}

These problems have had a direct effect on our operations. We have run two unplanned weekend shifts to recover lost production, paid expedited freight on four customer orders, and received a formal complaint from one of our largest customers. Our documented costs to date are summarized below; supporting records are enclosed.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
  \toprule
  Cost item & Amount (USD) \\
  \midrule
  Additional inspection and sorting labor & 18,450.00 \\

  Scrap and rework of nonconforming parts & 14,720.00 \\
  \midrule
  \textbf{Total}                          & \textbf{70,330.00} \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

We value the relationship between our companies, which goes back more than eight years, and we would much prefer to resolve these issues together. Accordingly, we request that Westbrook:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]
  \item provide a written corrective action plan, addressing both delivery performance and casting quality, no later than \textbf{October 21, 2026}, including root-cause analysis, specific actions, responsible persons and completion dates;
  \item respond in full to SCAR-2026-031 and SCAR-2026-044 by the same date;
  \item implement 100\% inspection of bore diameter on all shipments, at Westbrook's expense, until rejection rates have remained below 1.0\% for three consecutive months; and
  \item confirm in writing how Westbrook proposes to address the costs listed above, in accordance with Section~9.3 of the Agreement.
\end{enumerate}

I would also welcome a meeting at our Fort Wayne facility during the week of October 26 to review the plan with your operations and quality managers. Please contact me directly at (260) 555-0172 or \href{mailto:m.brennan@cardinalprecision.example}{m.brennan@cardinalprecision.example} to arrange a time.

Please be aware that if these matters are not satisfactorily addressed, Cardinal reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Agreement, including those under Section~12.2 (Termination for Cause). This letter is written without prejudice to those rights.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter. I look forward to your response.
