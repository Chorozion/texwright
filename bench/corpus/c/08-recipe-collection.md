# Weeknight Kitchen: Five Reliable Recipes

*A small collection for busy weeks, with nutrition per serving.*

All five of these can be made on a weeknight with ordinary supermarket ingredients. The nutrition numbers are estimates calculated from USDA FoodData Central values for the listed ingredients;[^est] brands and portion sizes will shift them a bit.

## 1. Sheet-Pan Lemon Herb Chicken

**Serves:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 35 min

One pan, no fuss. Thighs stay juicier than breasts at high heat, so they are worth using here.

### Ingredients

- 1½ lb (680 g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 lb (450 g) baby potatoes, halved
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 lemon (zest and juice)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 425 °F (220 °C). Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment.
2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tbsp oil and a pinch of salt. Roast alone for 10 minutes; they need a head start.
3. Meanwhile, mix the remaining 2 tbsp oil, lemon zest, half the lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Toss the chicken, broccoli, and onion in this mixture.
4. Push the potatoes to one side, add the chicken and vegetables in a single layer, and roast 20 to 25 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165 °F (74 °C).
5. Squeeze over the remaining lemon juice before serving.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 430 kcal | 36 g | 19 g | 3.5 g | 28 g | 5 g | 480 mg |

## 2. Smoky Black Bean Soup

**Serves:** 6 · **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 30 min

Canned beans make this a pantry meal. It thickens overnight and is better the second day.

### Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo, minced (optional, adds heat)
- 3 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- Juice of 1 lime
- Salt to taste

**To serve:** plain yogurt or sour cream, chopped cilantro, diced avocado, tortilla chips.

### Method

1. Warm the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion, carrot, and celery for 8 minutes, until soft.
2. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika, and chipotle; stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the beans and broth. Simmer 20 minutes.
4. Blend about half the soup with an immersion blender (or blend 3 cups in a regular blender and return it). This keeps some texture.
5. Stir in the lime juice and season with salt.

### Nutrition (per serving, without toppings)

| Calories | Protein | Fat | Saturated fat | Carbs | Fiber | Sodium |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 240 kcal | 13 g | 5 g | 0.7 g | 37 g | 13 g | 620 mg |

> **Tip:** Rinsing canned beans removes roughly a third of their sodium. If you are watching salt, use no-salt-added beans and season at the end.

## 3. Overnight Oats with Berries

**Serves:** 1 · **Prep:** 5 min · **Rest:** overnight

Make three or four jars on Sunday for the week's breakfasts.

### Ingredients

- ½ cup (40 g) rolled oats
- ½ cup (120 ml) milk of choice
- ¼ cup (60 g) plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
- Pinch of cinnamon
- ½ cup mixed berries (fresh or frozen)

### Method

1. Stir the oats, milk, yogurt, chia, honey, and cinnamon together in a jar.
2. Top with berries, cover, and refrigerate at least 6 hours.
3. Eat cold, or microwave for 1 minute. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

## 4. Miso-Glazed Salmon with Sesame Rice

**Serves:** 2 · **Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 15 min

The glaze takes two minutes and makes the dish. Broiling caramelizes it quickly, so watch the fish closely.

### Ingredients

**Salmon**

- 2 salmon fillets, about 6 oz (170 g) each
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger

**Rice and greens**

- ¾ cup (140 g) jasmine rice, uncooked
- 2 heads baby bok choy, halved
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 scallion, thinly sliced

### Method

1. Cook the rice according to the package directions.
2. Heat the broiler with a rack about 6 inches (15 cm) from the element. Whisk the miso, mirin, soy sauce, honey, and ginger.
3. Place the salmon on a foil-lined sheet and brush thickly with the glaze. Broil 7 to 9 minutes, until the top is browned and the center flakes.
4. While the salmon cooks, steam or sauté the bok choy for 3 minutes.
5. Toss the rice with the sesame oil and seeds. Serve with the salmon and bok choy, topped with scallion.

| Calories | Protein | Fat | Saturated fat | Carbs | Fiber | Sodium |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 560 kcal | 40 g | 20 g | 4 g | 52 g | 3 g | 820 mg |

## 5. Dark Chocolate Banana Bread

**Makes:** 1 loaf (12 slices) · **Prep:** 15 min · **Bake:** 55 to 60 min

The riper the bananas, the better: you want them heavily spotted or nearly black.

### Ingredients

- 3 very ripe bananas (about 1⅓ cups mashed)
- ⅓ cup (75 g) melted butter
- ½ cup (100 g) brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1½ cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
- ½ cup (85 g) dark chocolate chips (60% cacao or higher)

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 350 °F (175 °C) and grease a 9 × 5 in (23 × 13 cm) loaf pan.
2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl. Stir in the butter, then the sugar, egg, and vanilla.
3. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture and stir. Fold in the flour until just combined; a few streaks are fine.
4. Fold in the chocolate chips and pour into the pan.
5. Bake 55 to 60 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out with only moist crumbs. Cool 10 minutes in the pan, then on a rack.

| 210 kcal | 3 g | 8 g | 5 g | 33 g | 17 g | 2 g | 160 mg |
