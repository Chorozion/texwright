\section*{RC Low-Pass Filter}

The circuit below is a first-order RC low-pass filter. The cutoff frequency is $f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi RC}$.

\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\begin{circuitikz}
  \draw (0,0) to[sV, l=$v_{in}$] (0,3)
        to[R, l=$R$] (3,3)
        to[C, l=$C$, v^>=$v_{out}$] (3,0)
        -- (0,0);
  \draw (3,3) to[short, -o] (5,3);
  \draw (3,0) to[short, -o] (5,0);
  \draw (5,3) node[right] {$+$};
  \draw (5,0) node[right] {$-$};
  \node[ground] at (0,0) {};
\end{circuitikz}
\caption{RC low-pass filter.}
\end{figure}
