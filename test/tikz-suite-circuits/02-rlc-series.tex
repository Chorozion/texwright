\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{circuitikz}
\begin{document}

\begin{center}
\begin{circuitikz}[american voltages]
\draw
  (0,0) to[V, v=$V_s$] (0,4) % voltage source
  to[R=$R$, i>^=$i(t)$] (4,4) % resistor
  to[L=$L$] (4,0) % inductor
  to[C=$C$] (0,0); % capacitor
\end{circuitikz}
\end{center}

The series RLC circuit is described by
\[ L\frac{di}{dt} + Ri + \frac{1}{C}\int i\,dt = V_s. \]
