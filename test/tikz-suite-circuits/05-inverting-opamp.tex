\section{Inverting Amplifier}

\begin{center}
\begin{circuitikz}
  \draw
  (0, 0) node[op amp] (opamp) {}
  (opamp.-) to[R, l=$R_{in}$, -o] (-3, 0.5)
  (opamp.-) |- (-0.5, 2) to[R, l=$R_f$] (1.5, 2) -| (opamp.out)
  (opamp.out) to[short, -o] (2.5, 0)
  (opamp.+) -- (-1.2, -1) node[ground] {}
  (-3, 0.5) node[left] {$V_{in}$}
  (2.5, 0) node[right] {$V_{out}$};
\end{circuitikz}
\end{center}

The closed-loop gain is $A_v = -\dfrac{R_f}{R_{in}}$.
