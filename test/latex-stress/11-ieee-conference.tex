\section{System Model}
Each device $k$ has bandwidth $B_k$ and the rate is
\begin{equation}
R_k = B_k \log_2\left(1 + \frac{p_k |h_k|^2}{N_0 B_k}\right). \label{eq:rate}
\end{equation}
